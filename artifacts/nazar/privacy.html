Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

What Nazar stores

Nazar stores your journal entries locally on your own device, inside your browser or app storage.

What Nazar does not collect

  • Nazar does not require an account.
  • Nazar does not upload your journal entries to a server.
  • Nazar does not sell personal data.
  • Nazar does not include advertising or third-party tracking in the app experience.

Device storage

If you clear your browser or app data, your saved entries may be deleted. Because entries are currently local-only, they do not automatically sync across devices.

Future changes

If Nazar later adds accounts, cloud sync, payments, or analytics, this policy should be updated before those features are released.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Nazar owner or support address listed with the app.
